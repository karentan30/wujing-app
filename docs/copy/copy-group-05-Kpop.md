### Reddit（English）
**Finally figured out why my Pink Venom hand gesture looked wrong after two months of practice**

Uploaded my practice video to Dance Mirror. The report flagged two specific issues: wrist rotation angle was too small (not enough snap) and I was initiating the movement about half a beat late compared to the original.

One week of focused drill: wrist snap, then early initiation. Reuploaded — report confirmed both issues resolved.

For K-pop cover dancers: the precision analysis is genuinely useful for hand techniques where "it just looks wrong" doesn't tell you WHY it looks wrong.
